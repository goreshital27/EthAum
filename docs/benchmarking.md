# Competitive Benchmarking — Medical Tourism Platforms

> Research conducted November 2026 for the EthAum hackathon.  
> Only information observable on public websites is included. Where something could not be verified, it is marked "[unverified - confirm before submitting]".

---

## 1. Medical Tourism Co (medicaltourism.com)

**What it does:** A global directory and information hub that connects patients with accredited hospitals and clinics worldwide. Serves patients researching affordable or specialised treatments abroad, mostly from the US, Canada, and Europe.

**Patient journey:** Patient browses procedures and destinations → submits an enquiry with medical history → a case manager reviews records and returns a treatment plan with cost estimate → patient arranges travel (flights, visa, accommodation) → arrives for treatment → receives discharge summary and remote follow-up.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- Large, well-organised directory filtered by procedure, country, and accreditation.
- Educational content that helps patients understand what to expect.

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- Operates primarily as an informational directory — we did not find end-to-end booking or direct coordination on the public pages reviewed.
- No built-in quote comparison across hospitals was found on the public pages reviewed.
- We did not find a dedicated patient privacy dashboard or record access log on the public pages reviewed.
- Aftercare appears to be left to the individual clinic; we did not find platform-managed aftercare workflows.
- Testimonials are not verified against completed bookings.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Unverifiable patient testimonials and "success rate" claims with no source.
- Marketing-heavy content that can blur the line between information and promotion.

## 2. PlacidWay (placidway.com)

**What it does:** A global medical tourism marketplace connecting patients directly with clinics in 60+ countries ([PlacidWay Network](https://www.placidway.com)). Operates a direct-to-provider model (not a broker). Serves self-paying patients seeking dental, bariatric, cosmetic, orthopaedic, and fertility treatments.

**Patient journey:** Patient searches procedures and reviews clinic profiles with upfront pricing → submits a free quote request → clinic coordinator reviews records and schedules a virtual consultation → clinic sends a detailed treatment plan and quote → patient confirms appointment and pays a deposit directly to the clinic → travels (Booking.com integration for hotels) → treatment → leaves a review.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- Direct-to-provider model removes middleman friction.
- Upfront package pricing visible before enquiry.
- Built-in messaging and video consultation between patient and clinic.
- Booking.com integration for accommodation.

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- Quotes frequently exclude ancillary costs (flights, extended stay, medication); all-in pricing was not observed on public packages.
- No side-by-side quote comparison tool was found on public pages.
- Platform does not assume clinical liability — user reviews note patients must resolve issues directly with the foreign clinic.
- Limited post-op support once the patient returns home.
- Aggressive follow-up emails and calls after a quote request (reported in reviews).
- Quality of partner clinics varies widely despite accreditation screening.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Aggressive sales follow-ups that erode trust.
- Allowing unverifiable "before-and-after" photos without consent proof.

## 3. Medigo (medigo.com)

**What it does:** Originally a Berlin-based "Booking.com for medical tourism" marketplace (founded in November 2013 in Berlin; [Medigo Corporate Profile](https://www.medigo.com)). Has since pivoted to an independent enterprise-focused Third-Party Administrator (TPA) and healthcare assistance provider for insurance companies, MGAs, and self-insured employers ([Medigo Services](https://www.medigo.com)). The consumer marketplace is no longer the primary product.

**Patient journey (original model):** Patient searched procedures and compared accredited clinics → uploaded medical records → platform matched patient with specialists → received a treatment plan and cost estimate → platform coordinated visa, flights, transfers, interpreter → patient travelled and was treated → received discharge summary → remote follow-up.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- Single point of contact (dedicated case manager) from start to finish.
- Structured record upload and case-matching workflow.
- Concierge-level logistics coordination (visa, flights, interpreter).

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- Consumer marketplace appears to be de-emphasized or redirected toward B2B services.
- Trustpilot reviews report patients feeling "ghosted" after returning home with complications.
- Communication breakdowns between platform and clinics reported by reviewers.
- Accountability limitations — as an intermediary platform, clinical care and liability reside with third-party providers.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Over-promising hands-on support that the platform cannot actually deliver post-treatment.
- Pivoting away from patient-facing service without clear communication to existing users.

## 4. Healthtrip (healthtrip.com)

**What it does:** An India-based medical tourism platform and mobile app connecting international patients with hospitals across 7 countries: India, Turkey, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, the UAE, and Germany ([Healthtrip Global Destinations](https://www.healthtrip.com)). Serves patients seeking cardiac, orthopaedic, oncology, IVF, cosmetic, and transplant procedures.

**Patient journey:** Patient explores treatments on web or app → uses cost estimator to review package pricing → uploads medical records → AI tool parses reports, case manager routes them to specialists → receives treatment plans and quotes from multiple hospitals → selects a hospital and books → dedicated travel consultant handles visa, flights, transfers, accommodation → local "companion" meets patient at airport → treatment → post-op recovery support → medical summaries transferred for domestic follow-up → patient app for ongoing health tracking.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- AI-powered report parsing to speed up intake.
- Cost estimator tool with bundled package pricing.
- Multi-hospital quotes so the patient can compare.
- Mobile app with ongoing health tracking features.
- Local companion service for in-person support.

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- AI use is not clearly scoped — no visible disclaimer separating coordination AI from medical advice.
- Reviews on the platform were not verified against completed bookings on the public site [unverified - confirm before submitting - no public evidence found].
- Detailed patient record privacy dashboards and granular consent controls were not prominently displayed on public pages.
- Aftercare coordination relies on the patient proactively using the app.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Using AI in a way that could be mistaken for clinical guidance.
- Burying privacy and data-sharing terms in fine print.

## 5. Apollo Hospitals International (apollohospitals.com)

**What it does:** One of Asia's largest hospital groups, with JCI-accredited facilities across India. The International Patient Services (IPS) desk serves patients from 150+ countries ([Apollo Hospitals International Patient Services](https://www.apollohospitals.com/international-patient-services/)) seeking cardiology, oncology, orthopaedic, transplant, neuro, and fertility care.

**Patient journey:** Patient contacts IPS via website or WhatsApp → submits medical reports online → specialist team reviews and sends treatment plan with cost estimate → Apollo issues visa recommendation letter → patient travels (airport pickup arranged) → assigned a relationship manager and interpreter → treated at the hospital → receives discharge summary → teleconsultation follow-ups after returning home.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- Strong clinical brand backed by JCI accreditation.
- Dedicated relationship manager and multilingual interpreter.
- Teleconsultation follow-up as part of the standard offering.
- Customised dietary and comfort arrangements for international patients.

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- Single-hospital system — patients cannot compare competing hospital networks on the same portal.
- Public cost estimator was not found on the international portal; pricing appears available upon enquiry.
- We did not find a public referral or loyalty programme on the site.
- Detailed patient-facing medical record access controls were not prominently displayed on public pages.
- Review system is managed internally — no independent verification observed.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Presenting a single hospital's view as the only option (no competitive comparison).

## 6. Fortis Healthcare International (fortishealthcare.com)

**What it does:** A leading Indian hospital network with JCI/NABH-accredited facilities. The International Patient Division serves patients from the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, and CIS countries for cardiac, oncology, orthopaedic, neuro, and transplant procedures.

**Patient journey:** Patient contacts the international desk → shares medical reports online → receives a preliminary treatment plan, cost estimate, and second opinion → Fortis issues visa invitation letter → airport pickup → assigned a personal coordinator with language support and currency exchange assistance → treated → rehabilitation guidance → teleconsultation follow-ups.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- End-to-end concierge with a personal coordinator for each patient.
- Second-opinion service built into the standard flow.
- Teleconsultation aftercare.

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- Single-hospital network — no cross-provider comparison available on the portal.
- Public pricing calculators were not observed; quotes appear to be provided upon request.
- We did not find a public referral programme on the site.
- We did not find a patient-facing privacy dashboard or record access log on the public site.
- Reviews are managed by the hospital, not independently verified.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Opaque pricing that forces patients to submit records before seeing any cost indication.

## 7. Bumrungrad International Hospital (bumrungrad.com)

**What it does:** A premier private hospital in Bangkok, Thailand, with a reported 500,000+ international patients per year from 190+ countries ([Bumrungrad International Hospital](https://www.bumrungrad.com/)). Known for cardiology, orthopaedics, oncology, and wellness check-ups.

**Patient journey:** Patient contacts the hospital via website, email, or a global referral office → uploads medical history → specialist reviews and provides a cost estimate range → appointment scheduled before travel → visa and accommodation assistance → airport representative greets patient at Suvarnabhumi Airport → registration with free interpreter service (200+ interpreters covering 25+ languages, free of charge; [Bumrungrad Medical Coordination & Interpreters](https://www.bumrungrad.com/)) → consultation and same-day diagnostics → treatment in hotel-grade facilities → discharge with translated records → fitness-to-fly clearance → teleconsultation follow-ups from home.

**Strengths to ADAPT:**
- Airport counter at Bangkok's main airport — physical presence builds trust.
- Free interpreter service with 200+ interpreters covering 25+ languages ([Bumrungrad Medical Coordination](https://www.bumrungrad.com/)).
- Same-day diagnostics with on-campus labs.
- Discharge records translated into the patient's language.
- Hotel-grade inpatient rooms reduce patient anxiety.

**Weaknesses to IMPROVE:**
- Single-hospital model — no cross-hospital marketplace comparison.
- Cost estimates are typically provided as ranges rather than fixed, all-inclusive quotes; final price can change after in-person evaluation.
- We did not find a visible consent-based record-sharing dashboard on the public site.
- We did not find a public referral programme on the site.
- Patient reviews are curated by the hospital.

**Things to AVOID:**
- Providing cost "ranges" without clear upper-bound commitments.

## What EthAum Does Differently

1. **Verified facts vs. provider-reported claims.**
   Every hospital profile separates independently verified information (accreditations, facility audits) from provider-submitted claims (success rates, speciality descriptions). Patients always know which data has been checked and which has not.

2. **Consent-based record sharing with an access log.**
   Patients control exactly which hospitals can view their medical records and for how long. Every access, download, or view is written to an audit log the patient can review at any time — no silent sharing.

3. **Side-by-side, all-in quote comparison.**
   When a qualified case receives quotes from multiple hospitals, EthAum displays them in a standardised side-by-side format that includes procedure fees, hospital stay, travel, accommodation, and aftercare — so patients compare total cost, not just the headline number.

4. **AI for coordination only — never medical advice.**
   AI is used to summarise intake records, translate documents, and coordinate logistics. It never diagnoses, recommends treatments, or provides clinical opinions. Every AI-generated surface carries the label: *"AI-assisted coordination, not medical advice."*

5. **Verified reviews only after a completed booking.**
   Only patients who completed a booking and treatment through the platform can leave a review. This eliminates fake testimonials and ensures every review is tied to a real, traceable case.
